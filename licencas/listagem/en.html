Listagem

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This policy applies to Listagem, developed by André Luiz Nogueira.

At a glance

Your lists stay on your device. The app does not require an account. Advertising and purchases use Google services.

Data stored on your device

Lists, items, prices, purchase history and preferences are stored in the app’s local database. Import and export features let you manage your own files.

You can edit or delete data using the features in the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes local storage; exported files and any operating system backups or restores are managed separately by you and the system. Export anything you want to keep before switching devices.

Advertising and Google services

The app uses Google AdMob. The SDK may collect your IP address (from which approximate location may be inferred), device or advertising identifiers, interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostic and performance information. This data is used to display ads, measure results and prevent fraud.

See the Google Privacy Policy and the AdMob data documentation. Advertising identifier options are available in your Android privacy or advertising settings.

Consent

Listagem is distributed in Brazil. The current version does not include an advertising consent form for the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom.

In-app purchases

Purchases to remove ads or unlock features are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store card details. The app uses purchase status information to make purchased features available.

Permissions

  • Camera: used to scan codes; images from scanning are not stored or sent to the developer.
  • Files and storage: used to import and export data, depending on the Android version and the available file picker.
  • Internet: used by advertising and purchase services.

You can manage permissions in your device settings. Features that depend on a permission may be unavailable if you deny it.

Sharing, retention and your choices

We do not sell your data. Records you enter are not sent to a server operated by the developer. Google services process advertising and purchase data under their own policies and retention periods, including on infrastructure outside your country.

Local data remains until you delete it. Data you export or share is also handled by your chosen destination. For data processed by Google, see the contact channels in Google’s policy; for questions about the app, use the contact details below.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, please contact us so that we can look into the situation.

Contact and requests

André Luiz Nogueira is responsible for these apps and the processing of data described in this policy. For questions or requests to access, correct or delete data, write to contatopatapreta@gmail.com, specifying the app and the information needed to locate the record.

When you contact us, your email address and the contents of your message will be used to respond to your request.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when data handling practices change. The date above identifies the latest update.
