Delete your R3Forma account

Last updated: 31/10/2026

How to delete your R3Forma account (developer: André Luiz Nogueira) and what happens to your data. Details in the Privacy Policy.

In the app (the fastest way)

1. Open R3Forma (Android or https://patapreta.com.br/r3forma/). 2. On the home screen, tap the person icon ("Account") and sign in. 3. Tap "Delete account". 4. Type your e-mail to confirm. The account is deleted at once.

Without access to the app

Send an e-mail to contatopatapreta@gmail.com, from the account's e-mail, with the subject "Delete R3Forma account". We confirm the request and delete the account within 15 days.

What is deleted

The account's e-mail and (encrypted) password, the account id, the creation and last-use dates, every project and the price catalog kept in the cloud, the 3D models sent to the server, the Pro plan and purchase records tied to the account and the record of acceptance of the Terms and the Policy.

What stays, and for how long

Database backups: the data leaves them within 30 days, when the oldest daily copy is discarded. Server technical logs (such as the IP address), for each service's short term. Projects saved on the device and the .bproj, PDF and DXF files you kept stay with you: delete them or uninstall the app.

Subscription and ads

Deleting the account does not cancel the Pro subscription: cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions). Payment history and Google AdMob ad data stay with Google, under Google's policy.
