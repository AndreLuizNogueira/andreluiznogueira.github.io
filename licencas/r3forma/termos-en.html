What R3Forma is

R3Forma is a productivity tool for preliminary architectural studies: drawing plans, viewing them in 3D, estimating quantities and costs, and preparing files for a professional. It is not an architectural, construction or engineering design, not a technical report, and it does not replace a licensed architect or engineer, nor the responsibility record of whoever signs the design and the work.

No calculation is validated

The checks (building codes and technical standards such as NBR 5410, 8160, 5626, 6118, 6120, 9050 and 14718), the structural pre-design, the quantities and the budget are automatic estimates, based on simplified readings of the standards and not reviewed by an engineer. They may contain errors or omissions or be out of date, and they guarantee neither structural, electrical, plumbing or fire safety, nor compliance with standards, local law or the site conditions. A missing warning does not mean the design is right.

For a professional to assess

R3Forma exports the data in formats professionals use (PDF and DXF, the drawing format of AutoCAD and other CAD software), precisely so that a licensed architect or engineer checks, corrects, calculates and takes the technical responsibility. No construction, purchase or decision should rely only on what R3Forma shows.

Designs made with AI

Designs created or changed by an artificial intelligence through the R3Forma MCP server follow the same terms and may contain further errors. Review everything.
