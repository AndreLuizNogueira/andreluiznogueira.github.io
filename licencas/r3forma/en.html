Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31/10/2026 (version 4)

See also the Terms of Use and the End-User Licence Agreement.

Who is responsible

R3Forma is developed by André Luiz Nogueira, controller of the data described here. Contact for any privacy matter: contatopatapreta@gmail.com. [TO CONFIRM WITH A LAWYER: the data protection officer, or the small-agent exemption under ANPD Resolution 2/2022; the e-mail above is the service channel.]

Without an account: everything stays with you

Without signing in, R3Forma sends your projects nowhere. They stay in the .bproj files you save, wherever you choose, and in a recovery copy of the latest projects on the device or browser itself (on Android, encrypted with a key in the device Keystore). PDF, DXF and 3D images are also made on the device.

Account (optional)

To create an account we ask only for an e-mail and a password. We do not keep the password: the account service (Supabase) stores only an encrypted form of it. We also keep the account id, the dates it was created and last used, and a record of which versions of the Terms and of this Policy you accepted or read (document, version and date; nothing else). Account e-mails (activation, new password) are sent by Brevo, which receives your e-mail and the message text only to deliver it.

Sync (R3Forma Pro)

With an account and Pro, your projects (each one whole: plans, measurements, room names, prices and anything you write in them), your price catalog and the 3D models you import are kept in the cloud to reach your other devices. Projects and catalog are in the Supabase database, in the São Paulo (Brazil) region; 3D models in R3Forma's own server, rented from HostGator, in a data centre in Brazil (Vinhedo, SP). A backup copy of the database is made every day and kept on that same server in Brazil for 30 days. Each account sees only its own data: the database refuses any read or write of another account. If you write other people's data in a project (a client's name, an address), it goes along; handle it with the same care.

Pro subscription

On Android the subscription is made and billed by Google Play, with your Google account. We receive only Google's signed confirmation that it is active, to turn Pro on for your account; payment data stays with Google. You cancel it in Google Play.

Ads in the free plan

On Android the free plan shows Google AdMob ads, which may collect the advertising id, device information, the IP address and ad interaction data. Before the first ad the app shows Google's consent form where the law requires it; change your choice in "R3Forma Pro" → "Ad privacy". On the Web and on computers the ads are R3Forma's own and collect no data.

Website and MCP server

The website is served by R3Forma's server, which records connection data (such as the IP address) in technical logs for a limited time, to work and to protect itself. For those using the MCP server (projects made by AI, with an access key), requests and projects are kept in that key's own folder; download files stay 24 hours. The AI assistant used follows its own provider's policy.

What for, and on which legal basis

Account and sync: to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract, LGPD art. 7, V). Acceptance records: to show what was accepted and when (legal obligation and exercise of rights, art. 7, II and VI). Technical logs and security: legitimate interest in keeping the service working and protected (art. 7, IX). Personalised ads: consent (art. 7, I), which you can withdraw at any time. [TO CONFIRM WITH A LAWYER: the legal bases above.]

Who processes the data with us

Supabase (accounts and database; project in the São Paulo region), Brevo (account e-mails), HostGator (server of the website, the MCP, the 3D models and the backups) and Google (Play, AdMob and website components). These processors may be or have companies outside Brazil; the international transfer follows LGPD art. 33 [TO CONFIRM WITH A LAWYER: each processor's mechanism, e.g. contractual clauses]. We don't sell data or use it for anything other than what is described here.

For how long

Account and sync data stay while the account exists. Deleting the account erases them at once from the database and the models server; from the database backups, within 30 days, when the oldest daily copy is discarded. Technical logs follow each service's term. .bproj, PDF and DXF files you saved stay where they are, under your control.

Your rights and how to use them

You can confirm whether we process your data, access it, correct it, take it to another service (portability), ask for its deletion, know whom it is shared with and withdraw consent (LGPD art. 18). In the app: "Account" → "Export my data" (a file with everything the cloud keeps) and "Delete account". For the rest, write to contatopatapreta@gmail.com; we answer within 15 days. You can also complain to the Brazilian data protection authority (ANPD).

Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS); each account isolated in the database; passwords stored only in encrypted form by the account service; local database encrypted on Android; servers with restricted access. No measure is infallible: if an incident may put you at risk, we will tell you and the ANPD as the law requires.

Minimum age

R3Forma is a work tool, directed at people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children or teenagers; if we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, it and its data will be deleted.

Changes

When this policy changes, the app shows the new version at start. The date above is the last update. The use of the software itself is in the Terms of Use and the Licence Agreement (EULA).
